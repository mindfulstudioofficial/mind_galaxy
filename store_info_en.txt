English Store Description (Full)
Why not turn the thoughts floating in your mind into "stars" and grow your very own galaxy?

Mind Galaxy is a self-reflection app designed to visualize your daily emotions and insights, helping you find mental clarity.

【What you can do with Mind Galaxy】

Create Thought Stars: Categorize and record your thoughts into four types: Future, Past, Emotion, and Action.

Weekly Galaxy: Review your mental balance from the past week through a personalized constellation.

Meteor Shower Healing: Experience a special moment of relaxation with a beautiful meteor shower that triggers as your records grow.

Revisit Thoughts: Gently look back at your past self and see what you were thinking at that time.

【Recommended for those who】

Have a busy mind and tend to overthink.

Want to objectively observe their emotional patterns and behavioral habits.

Have struggled to maintain a consistent habit with traditional diaries or journaling.

Take a moment out of your busy daily life to observe the vast universe unfolding within your own mind.
