# MindGalaxy Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-21

MindGalaxy (the "App") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the App and how that information is handled.

## 1. Information We Collect

The App itself does not require account registration and does not directly collect personally identifiable information such as your name or phone number.

However, the App uses third-party services that may collect certain technical information automatically.

### Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses **Google AdMob** to provide advertisements.

AdMob may collect and process information such as:

- Advertising identifiers (for example, Android Advertising ID / Identifier for Advertisers)
- Device information (OS, device model, language, approximate location inferred from IP address, etc.)
- Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, and performance events)

This data is used for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and service improvement in accordance with Google policies.

For details, please refer to:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

## 2. Purpose of Use

Collected information is used for the following purposes:

- Displaying and optimizing advertisements
- Measuring app usage and improving app quality and stability
- Preventing abuse and fraudulent activity

## 3. Data Sharing with Third Parties

The App does not sell your personal information.
Data may be processed by third-party service providers (such as Google AdMob) to deliver the features described above.

## 4. Data Retention and Security

The App developer strives to handle data appropriately and securely.
Please note that information processed by third-party services is managed under each provider's own privacy policy.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.
If you believe inappropriate data collection has occurred, please contact us.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as needed.
If material changes are made, the updated policy will be reflected in the App and/or related distribution pages.
